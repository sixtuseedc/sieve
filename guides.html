Email Marketing Guides

Why List Hygiene Matters

Sending emails to invalid or outdated addresses damages your sender reputation. A clean list ensures your emails land in the inbox, not the spam folder.

How to Segment Your Leads

Different providers (like Gmail or Yahoo) treat incoming mail differently. By splitting your list by domain, you can tailor your outreach strategies effectively.

Security Tips

Always use browser-based tools like Sieve when handling sensitive contact lists. Because nothing is uploaded to a cloud server, your data remains yours alone.
