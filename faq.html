Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sieve really free?

Yes, Sieve is 100% free. We believe in providing simple tools to everyone without paywalls or hidden subscriptions.

Where does my data go?

Your data is processed locally in your browser. It never touches our servers, and we do not store any email addresses or text that you paste.

What file formats are supported?

Sieve supports .txt, .csv, .html, and .json files. You can upload them directly or paste raw text.

Can I use this for bulk marketing?

Yes, but always ensure you are complying with local anti-spam regulations like CAN-SPAM, GDPR, or CCPA. Sieve is a tool for organization, not for spamming.
