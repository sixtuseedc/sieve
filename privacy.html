Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026
Short version: Sieve runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your data. The text you paste and the emails you extract never leave your device.

1. Who We Are

Sieve ("we", "us", "our") is a free, browser-based email extraction tool available at sieve-ivory.vercel.app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our tool works.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many web tools, Sieve does not collect the following:

  • The text you paste into the tool
  • The email addresses you extract
  • Any files you upload
  • Your name, email address, or any personal information
  • Your IP address (beyond what is standard for web hosting)
  • Your browsing history

All text processing happens locally in your web browser using JavaScript. No content you enter is transmitted to our servers or any third-party service.

3. How the Tool Works

Sieve is a client-side web application. When you paste text or upload a file, that content is processed entirely within your browser's memory. The extracted email addresses are displayed back to you in the same browser window. When you close the browser tab, all of that data is gone. We have no access to it at any point.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Sieve may use the following limited tracking technologies:

  • Google AdSense: We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show you relevant ads based on your previous browsing activity. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads.
  • Basic analytics: We may collect anonymous, aggregated information about how many people visit the site and which features are used. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.

5. Third-Party Links

Sieve contains links to third-party websites including lender comparison tools and affiliate partners. When you click these links and visit external websites, those websites have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites.

6. Children's Privacy

Sieve is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove that information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@sieve-ivory.vercel.app
