How to Use Sieve Effectively

Sieve is designed to be the fastest way to pull contacts from messy data. Here is how to get the most out of it:

  • Bulk Extraction: Paste entire email threads, messy CSV exports, or raw web-scraped text. Sieve ignores the noise and extracts valid addresses.
  • Provider Sorting: Use the "Split" feature to separate leads by domain (Gmail, Outlook, etc.). This is essential for segmenting your marketing campaigns.
  • Privacy First: Sieve runs locally in your browser. No data is ever uploaded to a server, making it a secure way to handle sensitive contact lists.

Email Marketing Best Practices

Cleaning your list is the first step to high deliverability. Here is why you should maintain your contact data:

1. Improve Open Rates: By removing duplicates and invalid formats, you ensure your mail reaches real, active users.

2. Domain Reputation: Sending to invalid addresses can cause your domain to be flagged as spam. Always clean your data before importing it into your CRM.

3. Transparency: Respecting user privacy and being clear about how you process contact data is essential for modern marketing compliance.
